# Code of conduct
Welcome players and contributors of all experience levels. Be patient, respectful and constructive. Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Respect names, identities, privacy and creative ownership.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized harassment, doxxing and deliberate sharing of private material are not welcome. Do not retaliate against someone raising a concern.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or ban repeated/severe misconduct, applying proportionate action and considering context. Report concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact established before publication. A private reporting channel is still a publication prerequisite; do not post identifying details publicly while it is pending.
