Stack shows small layered edges. Fan is straight horizontal overlap with no arc/rotation. Horizontal/Vertical/Spread use actual sizes and gaps. Distribution uses dominant-axis centers and retains endpoints. Selection stays active for another formation or group drag; objects remain independent and keep their state. A local arrangement is one Undo transaction; multiplayer Undo is unchanged.

Demonstrate Stack → Fan on lands, horizontal creatures, vertical tokens, uneven row → Distribute, Stack → Spread.
